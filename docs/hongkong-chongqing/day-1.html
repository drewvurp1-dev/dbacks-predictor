Welcome to The Pearl of the Orient

Everyone lands. A settle-in day built around Victoria Harbour — don't fight the jet lag, lean into the evening.

Everyone converges Standby arrivals Sleep: Hong Kong

The shape of the day

Nothing is time-critical. The Peak and the ferry are both better at dusk anyway, so a late start is fine.

📍 Meeting point. Pick one before anyone takes off — hotel lobby at a set time, or the HKG arrivals hall. The two travelling from ORD may land on a different flight; agree who waits for whom.
Afternoon — get your bearingsPeak + harbour crossing
Victoria PeakMap The view. Restored Peak Tram up (buy a timed ticket online to skip the queue) or a taxi if the line is brutal. Walk the flat Peak Circle Walk for the quieter panoramas. Go late afternoon for daylight, sunset and the lights coming on in one visit. HKD 88–148 (~$11–19)
Star Ferry — Central → Tsim Sha TsuiMap The best HKD 5 in Asia — ten minutes across the harbour on a century-old ferry, skyline on both sides. Sit on the open lower deck. HKD 5 (~$0.65)
Evening — the harbour show8pm lights, then dinner
Symphony of LightsMap Nightly 8:00pm laser-and-light show across the harbour towers, ~10 min, best from the TST promenade / Avenue of Stars on the Kowloon side. Free. Free
Temple Street Night MarketMap Yau Ma Tei. Stalls, fortune tellers, and open-air dai pai dong seafood — clay-pot rice, typhoon-shelter crab, cheap beer. A relaxed first-night dinner that needs no reservation. HKD 150–250 pp (~$19–32)
If a flight slipsStandby contingency
The whole plan is Peak + ferry + dinner, and any subset of that still makes a good first night. If the group lands late, just do the promenade and Temple Street. Save the Peak for a clear morning later if you have to.
Day 1 · per person~HKD 450–750 (~$60–95)
