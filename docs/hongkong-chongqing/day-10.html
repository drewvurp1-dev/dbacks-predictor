Suits in the morning, culture in the afternoon, nothing in the evening — this is the last full day before a standby flight, and it's built light on purpose.

The shape of the day

Both halves of today sit a few minutes apart in central Yuzhong — no need to range far.

The plan is a ready-made Zhongshan suit (中山装 — the "Mao suit," named for Sun Yat-sen) for each brother, taken in on the spot rather than built from scratch, since there's no spare day left in the trip for full bespoke turnaround. Jiefangbei is the right neighborhood for it, but the specific shop isn't locked in — ask the hotel concierge to recommend and call ahead once you're there, and confirm same-day alteration is actually on offer before the whole group shows up.
Morning — suits, JiefangbeiOff-the-rack + on-the-spot alterations
Jiefangbei (解放碑)Map Chongqing's old central shopping district, and historically its tailoring district — close enough to Chaotianmen to walk if the hotel's the starting point. Ask for a 中山装 (Zhongshan suit) specifically; it's the correct term and what any tailor there will recognize immediately.
Buy ready-made, alter on the spot A finished suit takes days to build from nothing — alterations (waist, sleeve, length) run hours, not days, and still get each of you a suit that actually fits. Budget a few hours for the whole group. Suit + alterations, estimate only — confirm pricing locally
Afternoon — Three Gorges Museum & the Great HallLine 10 · Grand Hall Station (大礼堂站), exit 1
Chongqing China Three Gorges MuseumMap Bronze-age Ba-Shu artifacts, Han-dynasty sculpture, and the full Three Gorges Dam story, all under one roof. Free, but reserve a time slot online ahead if you can — some special exhibits charge separately. Free (reserve ahead)
The Great Hall of the People (人民大礼堂)Map Right across People's Square from the museum — one of Chongqing's most recognizable buildings, part Soviet-scale rotunda, part traditional Chinese roofline. The square itself is free; going inside the hall is a small add-on. ¥8 pp for the interior (~$1)
