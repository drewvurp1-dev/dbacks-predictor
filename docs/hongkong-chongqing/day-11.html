Mon · Jan 4 · Departure

The one day on this site with no fixed plan, on purpose. Seven of the nine are flying non-rev out of Chongqing (CKG) — the other two already have tickets home and aren't part of any of this.

The shape of the day

Nothing is bookable in advance. The job today is staying flexible and knowing the backup before you need it.

✈️ Nothing here is locked in. Standby means exactly that — don't plan anything time-critical around a departure time, and keep bags light enough to move fast if a seat opens on short notice.
Primary gateway — Hong Kong (HKG)CKG → HKG, 3x/day · 5 airlines
Why HKGMap Three nonstops a day across five airlines (Air China, Cathay Pacific, Hong Kong Airlines, Greater Bay, Chongqing Airlines) — a full flight just means trying the next one a few hours later, or switching carriers outright.
Onward from HKG Cathay Pacific alone flies nonstop to 7 US cities (Boston, Chicago, Dallas-Fort Worth, Los Angeles, New York JFK, San Francisco, Seattle), plus United and Delta. None of that capacity sits under the US–China bilateral flight cap currently throttling mainland gateways like Shanghai — Hong Kong flies its own, uncapped, aviation agreement with the US.
Backup — Seoul (ICN)CKG → ICN, daily except Tue/Sat · 2 airlines
If HKG doesn't pan outMap A slightly thinner first leg than Hong Kong's, but Korean Air alone flies nonstop to 11 US cities — the single biggest network of any option considered — plus Asiana. Same advantage as Hong Kong: Korea isn't capped by the US-China treaty either.
Considered, not itTokyo and Taipei — ruled out, not forgotten
Tokyo (NRT) The biggest US network of anywhere on this list in isolation — United alone runs 10 daily flights out of Narita. But CKG → NRT is only 4x/week on a single carrier, and Haneda isn't reachable nonstop from Chongqing at all. Too thin a first leg to risk it.
Taipei (TPE) China Airlines just launched a nonstop TPE → PHX flight — straight to the seven PHX-based travelers' home airport. Genuinely tempting, but it only flies Wed/Fri/Sun, and CKG → TPE skips Mondays, which is today. Worth revisiting if the departure date ever moves.
