Plus-Sized Park Hoppers

A full day at Hong Kong Disneyland — the only park with Mystic Manor and a World of Frozen — with the Big Buddha next door if the legs hold up.

Rope drop Full day in park Big Buddha if time

Before the gates

Small park by Disney standards, but New Year week is peak. A plan beats wandering.

🎟 Buy ahead. Park tickets online in advance, plus decide on Premier Access (paid ride skips) for the headliners. Put one person in charge of the group's tickets. Check the official calendar for opening time (often ~10:30) and the parade / fireworks schedule.
Hong Kong Disneyland — the run of showRope drop the newest, save shows for last
World of FrozenMap The newest land and the longest lines — hit it at rope drop. Wandering Oaken's Sliding Sleighs (family coaster) and Frozen Ever After (boat dark ride).
Mystic Manor The park's signature ride — a trackless dark ride that no other Disney park has. Do this before the crowd finds it.
Iron Man Experience & Ant-Man and The Wasp Tomorrowland's Marvel pair — a motion-simulator flight over Hong Kong and a shooter dark ride.
Adventureland Jungle River Cruise, the Rafts to Tarzan's treehouse, and the Festival of the Lion King stage show (worth the sit-down).
Lunch for nine Book a sit-down — Royal Banquet Hall (Fantasyland) seats groups and takes reservations; otherwise Comrade Sandwiches or the World of Frozen market stalls.
Afternoon parade → nighttime spectacular Grab a curb spot ~20 min before the parade. End the night with "Momentous", the castle projection-and-fireworks show — stake a viewing spot on the hub early. Park ticket ~HKD 640–750 (~$82–96)
Big Buddha / Ngong Ping — the optional addA half-day of its own
Ngong Ping 360 + Tian Tan BuddhaMap The 34m bronze Big Buddha, Po Lin Monastery, and the Wisdom Path, reached by a 25-minute cable car with glass-floor cabins over the hills. It's genuinely a half-day — realistic only if you skip the Disney night show, or split the group. One MTR hop from the Disney side: Sunny Bay → Tung Chung, then the cable car (or bus 23). Cable car round trip ~HKD 270–315 (~$35–40)
Honest call With a full Disney day, most groups won't have the energy. Consider moving the Buddha to a spare morning, or treating it as the plan if Disneyland doesn't appeal to everyone.
Getting there & aroundDedicated Disney train
MTR to Sunny Bay, change to the Disneyland Resort line — a short shuttle train with Mickey-shaped windows. Stroller and wheelchair rental inside the park entrance. Re-entry is fine, so a mid-day breather at the hotel is possible but eats an hour each way.
