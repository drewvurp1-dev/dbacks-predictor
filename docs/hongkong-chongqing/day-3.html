The Lucky Bankers vs. The Evil Dealers

A day trip to Macao — the biggest casino floors on earth, the gold-lit skyline, and the bar-and-restaurant scene once the chips are down. Back in Hong Kong tonight.

Ferry from HK Casinos + night lights Back to HK tonight

House rules

It's a day trip on purpose — tomorrow's Guilin train leaves from Hong Kong.

🛂 Passports both ways. Macao is a separate border. Bring them. Book the outbound ferry the night before for a group of nine, and keep an eye on the last sailing back (~22:00–23:00; some routes run 24h). Aim for a return by ~21:00 so nobody's wrecked for the early start.
Across the water~60–70 min · HKD ~175 each way
TurboJET or Cotai Water JetMap TurboJET from Sheung Wan → Macao Outer Harbour, or Cotai Water Jet → Taipa (drops you right by the casinos). First sailings ~07:00, then every 30–60 min. HKD ~175 (~$22) each way
The floorsCotai Strip + old-town glamour
The Venetian MacaoMap The largest casino floor on the planet, plus indoor canals with gondolas and a vast food hall — easy warm lunch for nine.
Wynn Palace & The Parisian Wynn Palace: ride the free SkyCab over the Performance Lake fountain show. The Parisian: go up the half-scale Eiffel Tower. Both on the free shuttle loop.
Grand Lisboa (downtown) Old-school Macao glamour — a gold lotus tower over the historic gambling district. Higher minimums and more serious tables than the Cotai megaresorts.
Table etiquette 21+, no photos on the floor, smart-casual. Main-floor minimums run higher than Vegas; look for the lower-limit rooms and the slot halls. Set a per-person budget before you sit down.
City night lightsBetween the tables
Senado Square & Ruins of St. Paul'sMap The UNESCO old centre — Portuguese wave-cobbles and pastel facades — lit and quieter after dark. The stone church facade at the top of the staircase is the city's signature shot. Free
Macau TowerMap 338m observation deck with a glass floor; the world's highest commercial bungee if anyone's serious. Good at night. MOP 165 (~$20)
Bars & restaurantsWhere to eat and drink
Taipa VillageMap Narrow lanes of Macanese and Portuguese places — African chicken, minchi, bacalhau, pork chop bun, and Lord Stow's egg tarts. The best food stop of the day. MOP 150–300 pp (~$19–37)
Drinks after The Cotai resort bars are the easy option; for something with more character, the lanes around Rua da Felicidade and the old Inner Harbour. HKD is accepted everywhere in Macao at par — no need to change money for a day trip.
Day 3 · per person~HKD 550–900 (~$70–115)

Gambling budget is your own — and NYE-week crowds on the floors are real.
