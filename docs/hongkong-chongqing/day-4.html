Mon · Dec 29 · Hong Kong → Guilin

Bullet train inland to Guilin — brushed-silver speed for three hours, then karst towers and river mist at the other end.

The pivot day

One night in Guilin turns a 7-hour rail slog into two easy legs and buys a river-town evening.

⏱ West Kowloon, 90 minutes early. You clear Hong Kong exit and Chinese entry immigration inside the station, and the 240-hour visa-free transit permit is issued here. Everyone needs their passport and the confirmed onward ticket out of the mainland. Full mechanics in the guide → Rail.
Leg 1 — West Kowloon → Guilin West~3–3.5 hrs · direct · up to ~350 km/h
The trainMap 580 km, no changes. Departures around 10:01 / 11:14 / 11:52 — take the ~10:00 for a full Guilin afternoon. Second class ~¥415–450 (~$58–63). 350 km/h is 217 mph, so the day's title checks out. Eat before the gate; on-train food is thin, but every car has boiling water for instant noodles.
Into town Guilin West is ~15 km / 25 min from the centre — grab two DiDis or taxis for the group.
Guilin afternoon — the river & the karstPick one headline, don't cram
Elephant Trunk HillMap Guilin's emblem — a rock arch on the Li River shaped exactly like an elephant drinking. Visible for free from the park across the water or Binjiang Road; the paid park gets you closer. ¥55–90 (~$8–13)
Reed Flute CaveMap A 240m illuminated limestone cavern, stalactites lit violet and green over a mirror pool. Kitschy lighting, real rock. ~1 hour. Hours/price unverified for the holiday — check locally. ¥110 (~$15)
The hikeShort and worth it — you only have the afternoon
Xianggong HillMap A 20-minute climb to the postcard Li River bend — best light early morning or late afternoon. It's ~1.5 hrs each way from town by car, so it works only if you skip the cave and have a driver. ¥30 (~$4) + car
In-town alternative Fubo Hill or Diecai Hill — short staircases straight up from the riverfront for a city-and-spires view, no drive needed. The Longji rice terraces are the famous hike but 2+ hrs each way — save them for a trip with more Guilin time.
Evening — lights on the waterThen noodles
Two Rivers & Four Lakes night cruiseMap ~1 hour through the lit canal ring around central Guilin — the twin Sun and Moon Pagodas, illuminated bridges, cormorant-fishing demos. The right way to spend a single Guilin evening. Book ahead. ¥165–235 (~$23–33)
Guilin rice noodles (mǐfěn) Rice noodles, fried peanuts, pickled long beans, preserved radish, thin beef, in a dark five-spice broth you top up yourself. A couple of dollars a bowl. Late supper after the cruise. ¥12–25 (~$2–4)
