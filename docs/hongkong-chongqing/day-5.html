Tue · Dec 29 · Guilin

A full day with nothing to catch — the karst peaks Guilin is famous for, a river cruise lit up after dark, and one real hike squeezed into the golden hour.

The shape of the day

No train, no border, no clock. Front-load the in-town stops, save the hike for the light it needs.

Morning — Solitary Beauty PeakCentral, quick, no rush
Solitary Beauty Peak & Jingjiang Princes' CityMap A single limestone tower rising straight out of a Ming-dynasty walled palace in the city centre — ~300 steps to a 360° view over Guilin's spires. Compact, central, and a good first look at the shape of the place before you're out on the water. ~1–1.5 hrs. ¥100–130 (~$14–18)
Midday — Elephant Trunk HillGuilin's emblem, on the river
Elephant Trunk HillMap A rock arch on the Li River shaped exactly like an elephant drinking — the city's postcard image. Visible for free from the park across the water or Binjiang Road; the paid park gets you closer and onto the water's edge. ¥55–90 (~$8–13)
Lunch riverside Easy to find a spot along Binjiang Road with the hill in view. Save the big meal for tonight's noodles.
Afternoon — the hikeBest light, and now you have time for it
Xianggong HillMap A 20-minute climb to the postcard Li River bend — the view that shows up on every Guilin postcard, best in the late-afternoon light. It's ~1.5 hrs each way from town by car, which is exactly why it never fit on a half-day — today it does. Two DiDis out, two back. ¥30 (~$4) + car
If the weather turns Reed Flute Cave is the fallback — a 240m illuminated limestone cavern, stalactites lit violet and green over a mirror pool, ~1 hour, entirely indoors. Swap it in for the hike if it's raining. ¥110 (~$15)
Evening — the boat tourThen noodles
Two Rivers & Four Lakes night cruiseMap ~1 hour through the lit canal ring around central Guilin — the twin Sun and Moon Pagodas, illuminated bridges, cormorant-fishing demos. The right way to close out a Guilin day. Book ahead for a group of nine. ¥165–235 (~$23–33)
Guilin rice noodles (mǐfěn) Rice noodles, fried peanuts, pickled long beans, preserved radish, thin beef, in a dark five-spice broth you top up yourself. A couple of dollars a bowl. Late supper after the cruise. ¥12–25 (~$2–4)
