Tue · Dec 30 · Guilin → Chongqing

Half a day of Guilin, then the last rail leg into Chongqing — a mountain city stacked in vertical layers over two rivers, pure neon after dark.

Getting the rest of the way

Pick a mid-to-late-morning departure: a proper Guilin morning, an evening arrival in the neon.

Half-day GuilinCentral, quick, before the train
Solitary Beauty Peak & Jingjiang Princes' CityMap A single limestone tower rising straight out of a Ming-dynasty walled palace in the city centre — ~300 steps to a 360° view over Guilin's spires. Compact and central; doable before a train. ~1–1.5 hrs. ¥100–130 (~$14–18)
Or just take it slow A riverside breakfast and a wander if the group's tired from Day 4. Check out, drop bags, head for Guilin West.
Leg 2 — Guilin West → Chongqing West~4 hrs if you book a fast one
The trainMap ~850 km. Fastest service (e.g. G320) is about 3h56m; many are 4.5–6 hrs with more stops — filter for the quick ones. ~20 trains a day, roughly 07:15–18:05. Second class ~¥390 (~$55).
Into the city Chongqing West connects to metro Line 5 and the Loop line; your hotel is likely 30–45 min out. Two DiDis with luggage is simplest.
First night in the neonA walk, not a grind
JiefangbeiMap The neon heart — a 1940s clock tower now ringed by a canyon of LED-clad towers. Walk it to get your bearings. Free
Hongyadong, from across the waterMap The gold-lit cliff of stilt-houses is the city's signature night view. See it from the Qiansimen Bridge or the opposite bank — don't fight the crowd inside, especially this week. Free
A warm-up hot pot A lighter one tonight to break the group in — tomorrow's the real event. Split pot, sesame-oil dip, milk tea on standby. ¥70–110 pp (~$10–15)
