Hot Pot and The Greatest Drone Show on Earth

A lighter day in the mountain city, a proper málà hot pot dinner, and the New Year's Eve drone show over the Yangtze from Jiangtan Park.

Daytime Chongqing Hot pot dinner Drone show · Jiangtan Park

Pace it for the night

Keep the daytime light — the evening is the whole point, and the crowds are real.

Daytime — the mountain cityPick two or three, don't march
Liziba StationMap Metro Line 2 runs straight through a 19-storey apartment block, with the station inside it. Viewing platform across the street; or just ride Line 2 along the Jialing cliffside — one of the world's great urban train rides. ¥2–5 (~$0.50)
Yangtze River CablewayMap A working commuter cable car across the Yangtze — ~5 minutes, big views up the gorge and back at the wall of towers. Queues are long midday; go early or buy a timed ticket. ¥30 (~$4)
Ciqikou Ancient TownMap A 1,000-year-old porcelain-trading port, now a warren of Ming-Qing lanes and street food — máhuā fried dough twists, spicy tangyuan, hand-pulled sugar. Go early before it packs. Free entry
Shibati (Eighteen Steps)Map A restored cliffside stairway district — teahouses, craftsmen, the layered-city feel with fewer crowds than Ciqikou. Free
Hot potChongqing invented it — book ahead for NYE
Málà hot pot Beef-tallow broth loaded with dried chilli and numbing Sichuan peppercorn. Order a yuānyāng split pot (spicy + mild) for the group, a sesame-oil-and-garlic dip, and tripe, luncheon meat, potato, lotus root, quail eggs. The spice is genuine — say "wēi là" (mild) if you mean it. Sweet soy milk or milk tea cuts the burn better than water. ¥90–140 pp (~$13–20)
Reserve NYE dinner slots go fast. Chains like Zhou Shi Mian Kuang, Houba, or Perilla handle groups; have the hotel call ahead for nine, early seating so you make the show.
The drone show — Jiangtan ParkGet there early
Jiangtan Park (江滩公园)Map Chongqing runs huge New Year drone-and-light shows over the Yangtze, and the riverbank lawns at Jiangtan Park are a prime viewing spot — open sky, the skyline behind the swarm.
⚠️ The crowds are the hazard, not the cold. Go early to claim a spot. Agree a meeting point and a lost-phone plan. Expect the metro to be jammed afterward — consider walking part of the way back or waiting it out. And confirm the show's location and time locally a day or two ahead; Chongqing moves these year to year and sometimes splits them across sites.
Midnight Stay put at the park, or a riverside bar with a view. Hongyadong and Jiefangbei will be shoulder-to-shoulder — admire from a distance.
