Fri · Jan 1 · Chongqing

A deliberately slow day after the drone show. Two stops, both an easy metro ride, neither one in a hurry.

The shape of the day

Late start on purpose. Ciqikou is best before the crowds find it; Hongyadong is better once the lights come up.

Morning — Ciqikou in daylightLine 1 · Ciqikou Station (磁器口站)
Ciqikou Ancient TownMap A 1,000-year-old porcelain-trading port, now a warren of Ming-Qing lanes and street food — máhuā fried dough twists, spicy tangyuan, hand-pulled sugar. One stop, direct, no transfer. Go before midday; it packs out fast on a holiday. Free entry
Breakfast, slowly Nothing time-critical. A teahouse table and a wander through the lanes beats a checklist today.
Afternoon / evening — Hongyadong, properly this timeLine 2 · Linjiangmen (临江门站), exit 2
HongyadongMap Day 6 only saw this stilt-house cliff complex from across the river at night. Today, actually walk it — eleven floors of shops, snack stalls, and viewing terraces stacked up the hillside. Exit 2 at Linjiangmen puts you right at it, ~11 min on foot. Free to enter
Stay for the lights It's built for both ends of the day — browse in daylight, then let the gold lighting come up around dusk before heading back. No need to rush off.
