Sat · Jan 2 · Chongqing

Two metro rides, one transfer, zero taxis — pandas in the morning, a retro-industrial arts park in the afternoon. Nothing about today needs a driver.

Today on the map

The shape of the day

One line each way, with a quick change at Lianglukou in between.

Morning — Chongqing ZooLine 2 · Zoo Station (动物园站), exit 1
Giant & red pandasMap Exit 1 lets out directly at the gate — no walk, no transfer confusion. Go earlier in the morning, which is when the pandas are actually awake and moving rather than napping through the midday cold. Zoo entry ~¥10–30 pp (~$1–4)
Afternoon — Eling Park & Erchang Creative ParkLine 1 · Eling Station (鹅岭站)
Change at Lianglukou From the Zoo, one transfer at Lianglukou (where Lines 1/2/3 all meet) gets you onto Line 1 toward Eling — no taxi needed either way today.
Erchang Creative Park (贰厂文创公园)Map A former state print factory turned retro-industrial arts park — murals, cafes, old factory signage repurposed into something genuinely stylish. Free to wander. Free
瞰胜楼 — the viewing building Right next door in Eling Park proper: a lookout over where the Yangtze and Jialing both come into view at once. Good spot to slow down before heading back.
Day 9 · per person~¥10–30 (~$1–4)
