Before You Go

⚠️ Mainland China entry. Hong Kong & Macao are visa-free for US passports. The mainland (Guilin, Chongqing) is not — the plan enters on the 240-hour visa-free transit, boarding the train at Hong Kong West Kowloon. It works for this routing but has hard conditions, and a 10-year tourist visa is the zero-risk alternative for nine people. Full checklist in the printable guide → Tips. Decide the route by early October.

Practical

The parts that don't change day to day. Details in the full guide.

Getting there & the rail legsFlights are standby — nothing locked
Into Hong KongSeven travelling from PHX, two from ORD, all converging at HKG around Dec 26. On standby, so arrival times are genuinely up in the air — build in a soft buffer before anything time-critical.
HK West Kowloon → GuilinDirect G-train, ~3–3.5 hrs. This is where you clear mainland immigration and the 240-hour transit permit — arrive 90 min early. Rail detail →
Guilin → ChongqingDirect G-train, ~4 hrs (pick a fast one; some are 5–6). Book both legs the moment the 15-day window opens — holiday week sells out.
Getting aroundThree payment worlds in one trip
Hong KongOctopus card (add to phone wallet) for MTR, buses, trams, Star Ferry. Contactless cards work everywhere. Uber works.
MainlandSet up Alipay + WeChat Pay with a foreign card before you fly — it's how you pay for everything. DiDi for taxis. Carry ¥500–1,000 cash backup.
Before you leaveInstall and test a VPN at home — Google, Instagram, WhatsApp are blocked on the mainland. Amap or Apple Maps for navigation; Google Maps is useless there.
MoneyCNY ~¥7.15 · HKD ~7.8 to $1
Mainland is cheap once you're moving (~$15–30/day of food eating well); Hong Kong and Macao run $40–70/day. Rough ground budget, flights and hotels aside: ~$600–1,000 per person. Full line items in the guide → Budget.
Weather & packingLate Dec — no snow anywhere
Hong Kong / MacaoMild, ~15–20°C, humid, light rain possible. Jacket + shell.
GuilinCold and damp, ~5–12°C, often misty. Real warm layer.
Chongqing~6–12°C, heavily overcast ("Fog City"), rarely freezing. Warm layers, waterproof shoes for wet stone steps.
