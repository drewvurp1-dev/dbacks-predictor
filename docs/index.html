Day-by-Day Itinerary
8 days across Tbilisi, Mtskheta, and Gudauri. NYE in the city, slopes after the rush clears.
⚠️ Standby note: Dec 31–Jan 2 has high load factors on nearly every route. Fly into TBS by Dec 26–27 and depart no earlier than Jan 5–6. BA LHR–TBS is your last oneworld link — don't plan to standby home on Jan 1–2.
1
Dec 27 · Saturday
Arrival + First Taste of Tbilisi
Culture
Get off the jet lag, get into the sulfur baths, and let Old Town do its thing. Don't fight the time difference — lean into the evening and let Tbilisi's atmospheric streets do the work.
Abanotubani Sulfur Baths (Chreli Abano)Book a private room (60–80 GEL). Ask for the "Kisa" scrub treatment. Some rooms have rooftop pools with Narikala views at night.
Shardeni Street & Erekle IIDinner in Old Town — Shardeni Street is the beating heart of Tbilisi's evening scene, packed with wine bars, cocktail bars, and Georgian restaurants with live music spilling onto the street. A perfect first-night warm-up. Slightly more tourist-facing than Vera/Sololaki but the atmosphere is electric, especially in festive season. Wine by the glass: 8–15 GEL.
2
Dec 28 · Sunday
Tbilisi Deep Dive
Culture Nightlife
Old Town, the fortress, and the Christmas Village by day — then live music in the evening. The city is beautifully decorated, festive mood runs Dec 12 through Jan 14.
Narikala FortressTake the cable car from Rike Park (2.5 GEL). 4th-century fortress with panoramic views of the Mtkvari River and Old Town. Free to explore at the top.
Sioni Cathedral + Anchiskhati BasilicaTwo of Tbilisi's most important religious sites — oldest surviving church in the city. Go early morning for no crowds.
First Republic Square / Christmas VillageChristmas Village runs Dec 12–Jan 14. Mulled Saperavi wine, market stalls, live music — and where the main NYE Gala Concert will be on Dec 31.
🎷 Evening: Jazz Cafe SingerLive jazz and blues in a beautifully restored historic building in Old Town — cozy, soulful, and the most relaxed entry point into Tbilisi's music scene. No face control, no dress code, no agenda. Just Georgian wine and good music. Open from around 8 PM.
3
Dec 29 · Monday
Wine, Food, Music & Sololaki
Food & Wine Nightlife
Slow morning at the baths, afternoon food market, evening wine bar crawl into live music on the river — then the late-night khinkali run that started this whole conversation.
Orbeliani Baths (Abanotubani)The blue mosaic exterior is iconic for photos. Morning session before exploring. Entrance 60–80 GEL private room.
Dezerter BazaarTbilisi's chaotic, beautiful covered market. Spices, churchkhela (Georgian snickers: walnuts in grape juice), cheese, dried fruit. Bring cash.
Sololaki Wine Bar Crawl — Vino Underground + neighbors19th-century Art Nouveau neighborhood. Underground brick cellars serving regional qvevri pours. Vino Underground is the anchor — one of Georgia's best natural wine lists in a candlelit cellar. Side streets off Gorgasali Square hide additional gems. Budget 8–15 GEL/glass.
🎸 Mtkvarze — Live Music on the RiverbankTbilisi's best live music venue for mixed genres — Georgian folk acts, jazz, and occasional rock, all on the banks of the Mtkvari. Informal and local without the tourist-bar pricing of Shardeni. The setting — Metekhi Church lit up on the cliff above, the river below — is remarkable. Open from ~8 PM.
🥟 MUST: Late-Night Khinkali RunThe canonical post-bar food in Tbilisi. Fat, pleated Georgian dumplings filled with spiced meat broth — hold by the topknot, bite a small hole, drink the broth, eat the rest. Leave the doughy knot on the plate (it's a counter of how many you've had). Late-night khinkali specialists near Fabrika and in the Marjanishvili area exist almost entirely to feed people coming off a long night. Open until 2–3 AM or later.
4
Dec 31 · Wednesday
New Year's Eve in Tbilisi 🎆
Major Event
New Year's Eve is bigger than Christmas in Georgia. The whole city becomes a party — elaborate supras (feasts), citywide fireworks, and raucous celebrations that stretch for weeks. Book your restaurant well in advance — this sells out weeks ahead.
Traditional Supra FeastPre-book a restaurant for an elaborate Georgian feast. Khachapuri, khinkali, grilled meats, wine, and toasts led by a Tamada (toastmaster). Book 3–4 weeks in advance — these sell out.
First Republic Square Gala Concert + Midnight FireworksMain public celebration. Big Georgian pop stars, countdown, fireworks. Metro, buses, and Rike ropeway run FREE until 3 AM.
🎧 After Midnight: Bassiani or Khidi (Optional)New Year's Eve is one of Bassiani's biggest nights. If you're still standing after the fireworks and want to experience one of the world's great techno clubs — this is your shot. Bassiani opens at midnight and runs until 10–11 AM. Register online in advance at bassiani.com — don't just show up. Dress dark and unpretentious. No photos allowed (stickers taped over your camera lens at the door). Arrive after 2–3 AM for no queue. Entry 20–40 GEL.

If Bassiani feels like too much of a commitment, Café Gallery in Old Town is more accessible with a similar electronic vibe, or Modus has a younger local crowd and less intimidating door.
🥟 Post-Club Khinkali (3–5 AM)This is mandatory. Marjanishvili area near Fabrika. You'll know you're in the right place when half the tables are filled with people still in club clothes.

Jan 2 · Friday
Bedoba — Day of Destiny
Culture
January 2nd is Bedoba ("Day of Luck") — Georgians believe how you spend this day sets the tone for the entire year. Keep it calm. Spa morning, afternoon wine bar, pack for Gudauri.
Morning Sulfur BathA slow soak at Abanotubani before heading to the mountains — the perfect Bedoba activity.
Pack for GudauriGoTrip driver booked for Jan 3. Confirm transfer the evening before. Bring cash — smaller restaurants and rental shops in Gudauri are cash-only.
6
Jan 3 · Saturday
Mtskheta Day Trip + Transfer to Gudauri
UNESCO Sites
GoTrip driver stops at Mtskheta en route to Gudauri. Georgia's spiritual heart and ancient capital — home to two UNESCO World Heritage sites. A guide genuinely matters here; without one, they're "just pretty stones."
Svetitskhoveli Cathedral, MtskhetaUNESCO site. Georgia's holiest cathedral, reportedly containing Christ's robe. 11th century. Stunning interior frescoes. ~20 min from Tbilisi. Visit with a guide (~€30–40).
Jvari MonasteryPerched atop a cliff overlooking the confluence of the Aragvi and Kura Rivers. 6th century, UNESCO site. 590–605 AD architecture. Breathtaking views.
Ananuri Fortress (en route)Free admission. Fortified church complex on a reservoir. Two churches inside the walls — one with vivid 17th-century frescoes. ~1 hour stop on the way to Gudauri.
Arrive Gudauri Ski ResortAfternoon arrival. Check in, afternoon ski run, après-ski bar. The NYE rush has cleared — resort is yours.
7
Jan 4 · Sunday
Full Ski Day — Gudauri
Skiing
80km of runs, modern lifts, snow depth averaging 1–2m. Ski lifts run 10am–5pm. Après-ski starts early at the resort bars. Budget ~170–250 GEL/person for lift pass, gear rental, food, and drinks.
Gudauri Ski ResortLifts open 10am. Day pass 50–70 GEL. Gear hire 40–60 GEL. Northern slopes have the best powder and longest season. Consider the Kobi-Gudauri cable car for panoramic Kazbek views.
Heli-Skiing (Optional)Gudauri is one of the most affordable heli-ski operations in the world. Book through a local operator — or enquire at the resort the evening before.

Jan 5 · Monday
Final Ski + Return to Tbilisi
Skiing
Half day skiing, then the 2-hour GoTrip transfer back to Tbilisi for departure. If Orthodox Christmas (Jan 7) timing works, consider extending to catch the Alilo Procession.
Return Transfer to TbilisiGoTrip driver, ~$60/car, 2–2.5 hours. Confirm driver the night before and check road conditions on the Georgian Road Department Facebook page before departing.
Bonus: Jan 7 — Orthodox Christmas Alilo ProcessionIf extending the trip, the Alilo Procession starts ~2pm from First Republic Square and ends at Sameba Cathedral. Thousands singing through Old Tbilisi. Not to be missed.
Getting There
PHX → TBS via oneworld partners. All AA metal to LHR, then BA to Tbilisi.
Standby strategy: Position into TBS Dec 26–27 with buffer. Return Jan 5–6 or later. Avoid trying to standby home Jan 1–2 — load factors are brutal everywhere during that window.
Recommended Routing (Oneworld)
PHX → Gateway → LHR → TBS
PHX to JFK, ORD, or DFW on AA metal. Then AA transatlantic to LHR. Final leg TBS on British Airways (oneworld). All legs covered under oneworld benefits after PHX.
Alternate Routing (Oneworld)
PHX → Gateway → DOH → TBS
Qatar Airways (oneworld) operates year-round DOH–TBS service. Use if positioning to a DOH-connected gateway makes more sense on a given date. More complex than the BA option.
Not Recommended
Cape Verde (SID/SAL)
No oneworld carrier serves Cape Verde. Air France (SkyTeam) and Cabo Verde Airlines are the primary options — would require buying a revenue transatlantic ticket. Georgia wins on routing.

Airport Info

Tbilisi International Airport (TBS) — Shota Rustaveli Airport. Located ~17km east of the city center. Bolt from airport to Old Town: 25–35 GEL (~$9–13 USD). Metro (red line) also connects the airport to the city center cheaply. Avoid curbside taxi touts.
Getting Around Georgia
No unified national network — you mix tools depending on the leg. Affordable once you know the system.

Within Tbilisi

🚇 Metro
Two lines, efficient, under 1 GEL/ride. Runs free until 3 AM on New Year's Eve. Best for cross-city trips.
📱 Bolt
Go-to ride app. Like Uber. Upfront pricing, Apple Pay. No language barrier. Always use app over street taxis.

Intercity

🚗 GoTrip.ge
Best for day trips & transfers. Fixed price locked at booking. Can stop anywhere. English interface. Book in advance.
🚐 Marshrutka
Minibus network. Cheapest option (5–25 GEL). Leave when full — no fixed schedule. Cash only to driver. Depart from Didube (north/west) or Ortachala (east).
Key distinction: Didube and Ortachala bus stations are absolutely nowhere near each other — confirm which station serves your route before leaving your hotel.

Road to Gudauri — Safety

Safe with a driver, more nuanced if self-driving. The Military Highway (Tbilisi → Gudauri) is well-maintained and regularly cleared. Winter tires are mandatory. Road closures happen on the section north of Gudauri (Gudauri → Kazbegi), not between Tbilisi and Gudauri. GoTrip drivers are vetted and equipped. Check GeoRoad Facebook Page or call +995 322 313 076 for real-time road status.

Tips & Practical Info
Everything you need to know before you go.

Holidays & Calendar

🎄
Dec 25 — Western Christmas
Celebrated but subdued. Christmas Village at First Republic Square is already in full swing (runs Dec 12–Jan 14). Mulled wine, markets, live music nightly.
🎆
Dec 31 — New Year's Eve (Biggest night of the year)
NYE is bigger than Christmas in Georgia. Elaborate family supras (feasts), citywide fireworks at midnight, free public transport until 3 AM. Book restaurants 3–4 weeks ahead minimum. Christmas Village hosts the main Gala Concert.
🍀
Jan 2 — Bedoba (Day of Luck)
How you spend this day is said to define your whole year. Georgians keep it calm — brunch, spa, relaxed gatherings. Perfect excuse to do a morning bath session before heading to the mountains.
⛪
Jan 7 — Orthodox Christmas + Alilo Procession
Georgia uses the Julian calendar — Christmas falls on Jan 7. The Alilo Procession starts at ~2pm from First Republic Square, ending at Sameba Cathedral. Thousands chanting through Old Tbilisi's cobblestone streets. One of Georgia's most remarkable public events. Consider extending the trip to catch it.

Money & Payments

💳
Currency: Georgian Lari (GEL)
1 USD ≈ 2.7 GEL. Cards widely accepted in Tbilisi hotels, restaurants, and Gudauri lift ticket offices. Smaller mountain restaurants and marshrutka drivers are cash only. Withdraw GEL from Tbilisi ATMs before heading to the mountains. Bring backup cash from PHX.
🚕
Never take a curbside taxi
Unmetered, tourist pricing is common. Always use Bolt in cities. Airport curbside taxi touts are especially aggressive — walk past them to Bolt pickup zones or metro.

Food & Drink Must-Tries

🧀
Khachapuri
Georgia's iconic cheese bread. The Adjarian variety (Adjaruli) is a boat-shaped bread loaded with melted cheese, butter, and a raw egg cracked in at the table. Non-negotiable order.
🥟
Khinkali
Georgian soup dumplings. Hold by the knob, bite a small hole, suck the broth, then eat the rest. Do not eat the knob — it's a handle, not food. Pasanauri town on the drive to Gudauri is famous for its khinkali; stop for lunch.
🍷
Qvevri Wine
Georgia has 8,000 years of winemaking history. Qvevri wines are fermented underground in large clay vessels — especially the amber/orange wines made from white grapes with skin contact. Rkatsiteli and Mtsvane are the classic Georgian white varieties; Saperavi is the signature red. Always order a degustation in a Tbilisi wine cellar.
🌰
Churchkhela
Georgia's "Snickers" — walnuts or hazelnuts threaded on a string and dipped in thickened grape juice. Found at every market. Buy from Dezerter Bazaar, not tourist shops.

Packing for Winter

🧥
Tbilisi: Chilly European winter
December averages 7°C (45°F) highs, dropping to -2°C (28°F) overnight. January is colder: ~4°C (39°F) highs. Pack a real winter coat, warm layers, and waterproof shoes for cobblestone streets. About 51% clear/sunny days in December — don't expect constant gray.
⛷️
Gudauri: Full mountain kit
Ski gear rentable on-site (40–60 GEL/day). Bring your own helmet if you have one — rental helmets at mountain resorts worldwide are hit or miss. Sunglasses and SPF 30+ essential at altitude even in winter. Layers: resort base area is warmer than on-piste.
👗
Modest dress for religious sites
Shoulders and knees covered for Mtskheta, Ananuri, and any churches. Women should have a scarf for head covering inside active churches. This applies to Jvari Monastery, Svetitskhoveli Cathedral, and Gergeti Trinity if you add Kazbegi.

Nightlife

🍷
Wine Bars — Sololaki & Vera (Start Here)
The best casual nightlife in Tbilisi. Underground brick cellars serving natural qvevri wines by the glass (8–15 GEL). Vino Underground in Sololaki is the anchor — one of Georgia's best natural wine lists in a candlelit cellar. Side streets off Gorgasali Square have additional gems. The Vera district is more local and slightly less polished — both worth exploring. Bars open ~7 PM and run late.
🎷
Jazz Cafe Singer — Live Jazz in Old Town
Live jazz and blues in a beautifully restored historic building. Cozy, soulful, no agenda. The most relaxed music venue in the city — no face control, no dress code, just Georgian wine and good music. Best on weekends. Open from ~8 PM.
🎸
Mtkvarze — Live Folk, Jazz & Rock on the River
Tbilisi's best live music venue for mixed genres — Georgian folk acts, jazz, and rock on the banks of the Mtkvari. Informal and local without tourist-bar pricing. The setting is remarkable: Metekhi Church lit up on the cliff above, Old Town on both sides. Open ~8 PM.
🎧
Bassiani — The Techno Club (Optional, Commitment Required)
Ranked alongside Berghain as one of the world's great techno clubs. Housed in the former Olympic swimming pool under Dinamo Arena stadium — a massive concrete space with an immersive sound system. Fri & Sat only, midnight to 10–11 AM.

Rules: Register at bassiani.com a few days before — don't just show up. Dress dark and unpretentious (black hoodie, casual). Camera taped over at the door — no photos, no exceptions. Act sober in line. Arrive after 3 AM to skip the queue. Entry 20–40 GEL.

It's not for everyone — it's pure techno, 10+ hours, very serious about the music. But if you want to see why Tbilisi has a global reputation, this is it.
🌉
Khidi — Under the Bridge
Occupies the underside of the Vakhushti Bagrationi Bridge over the Mtkvari, with outdoor decks and a harder industrial aesthetic. Similar electronic music programming to Bassiani with a slightly less intimidating door. Good backup if Bassiani is too packed on NYE.
🎨
Café Gallery — Most Accessible Club
Art space meets electronic music in Old Town. Similar vibes to Bassiani but more accessible door policy — a good first-timer entry point into Tbilisi's club scene. Good for NYE if Bassiani feels like too much of a commitment.
🥟
🌟 MUST: Late-Night Khinkali Run
The canonical post-bar, post-club food in Tbilisi. Late-night khinkali specialists near Fabrika and in the Marjanishvili area are open until 2–3 AM specifically to feed people coming off a long night. How to eat them: Hold by the topknot, bite a small hole, drink the broth first, then eat the dumpling. Leave the doughy knot on the plate — it's a counter of how many you've had. Do not use a fork. Georgians will notice.
💰
Nightlife Budget
A full evening — dinner, wine bar, and club — runs 80–150 GEL/person ($29–55 USD). Wine by the glass: 8–15 GEL. Craft beer: 8–12 GEL. Cocktails: 15–25 GEL. Club entry (Bassiani/Khidi): 20–40 GEL. Late-night khinkali: ~5 GEL each. One of the best-value nightlife cities in Europe.

Accommodation

🏨
Hilton Garden Inn Tbilisi Riverview ✅
Ranked #6 of 498 hotels in Tbilisi (4.7★). Located near Old Town, within walking distance of Narikala Fortress, Metekhi Church, and the sulfur baths. 10 minutes from the historical center, 15 minutes from TBS airport. Mom's friend & family discount: ~30–50% off (~$50–80/night est.).

⚠️ Book immediately — NYE availability disappears fast. Confirm how the discount is applied; you may need to call the hotel directly rather than booking online.

Avoid: Hilton Garden Inn Tbilisi Chavchavadze — it's in the Vake district, further from Old Town, and requires a Bolt for every activity.
⛷️
Gudauri — Book Separately
The HGI deal covers Tbilisi nights only. For Gudauri (Jan 3–5), book directly at the resort. Budget 120–200 GEL/night (~$44–74). Gudauri Lodge is the best option — 4.3★, 5-star hotel with spa, pool, and sauna. Also consider Gudauri Hut (4.5★, cozy and family-friendly with free breakfast). Book early — January fills up after the NYE rush clears and ski season hits full stride.
