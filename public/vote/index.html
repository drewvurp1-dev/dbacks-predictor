How does ranked-choice work?

Everyone put the destinations in order. Only your top surviving choice counts at any moment.

If nowhere has more than half the votes, the destination in last place is knocked out — and everyone who had it first has their vote moved to their next choice instead. That repeats until somewhere holds a majority.

It means you could rank a long shot first without wasting your vote: if it goes out, your ballot still has a say between the ones left.
